# BRAND — Hooksmith

## Name rationale

**Hooksmith** — a smith forges things; hooks are the thing I forge. The name is an
intentional parallel to `skillsmith` (which owns skills). Where skillsmith adds
_capabilities_, hooksmith adds _behavioral guarantees_: "this will run when X happens,
and we can prove it."

The double meaning is deliberate: a hook-smith is also someone who is deft with hooks —
the kind of hook that catches you before you fall (a PreToolUse block on `rm -rf`).

## Symbolic connection to the workflow

The workflow is **lifecycle control**. Every session, tool call, compact, stop — these
are moments when something should happen automatically. Hooksmith is the
blacksmith who forges the links in that chain: each link a named recipe, each chain
a settings.json hooks block, each forge-run a verified proof that the chain holds.

## Tagline

**"The hooks that make every harness behave."**
(Alt: "Forge your lifecycle, verify your hooks." / "Behavior as a deliverable.")

## Look & feel

Energy design system — warm black (`#141312`) base, Instrument Serif display + Poppins
body, dark mode. Signature accent: an **iron-blue → electric-white gradient** (forge
heat → tested, clean). Motif: a hammer striking a chain link (forging + connecting).

Distinct from:
- Darwin's evolution-green (Hooksmith is about reliability, not exploration)
- Armory's machined amber (Hooksmith is about behavior, not inventory)
- Helios's solar gold (Hooksmith is about guarantees, not earning)

## One-liner (internal)

Hooksmith is the lifecycle-hooks employee: **generator** that emits the `hooks` block,
**linter** that validates it, and **test harness** that proves it fires.
